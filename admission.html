Admissions & Enrollment

Join Aliaga National High School - Excellence in Education with DepEd-Compliant Enrollment Procedures

DepEd-Compliant Enrollment Requirements

For New Students (Grade 7)

Form 138 (Report Card)

Original copy with at least 75% general average from Grade 6

PSA Birth Certificate

Authenticated copy (photocopy for verification, original for presentation)

Certificate of Good Moral Character

Issued by the previous school principal or adviser

4 copies of 2x2 ID Pictures

Recent identical photos with white background, name at the back

For Transferees (Grades 8-10)

Form 138/Report Card

With general average of at least 75% from previous school

Certificate of Transfer Credentials

Official transfer credentials from previous school

Student Permanent Record (Form 137)

Original copy from previous school

Additional Requirements (If Applicable)

Barangay Clearance

Proof of residency in Aliaga, Nueva Ecija

Immunization Record

For Grade 7 entrants (Measles, Rubella, Tetanus)

Special Child Identification Card

For students with special needs (PWD ID if applicable)

Special Program Requirements

Additional documents for specialized school programs

Step-by-Step Enrollment Process

1

Online Pre-Registration

Complete the online enrollment form available on our website or visit the school registrar's office.

2

Document Submission

Submit complete requirements to the Registrar's Office during assigned schedule.

3

Evaluation & Assessment

School personnel will evaluate documents and conduct placement tests if necessary.

4

Medical Examination

All new students undergo medical check-up at the school clinic.

5

Section Assignment

Receive section assignment and class schedule based on assessment results.

6

Parent Orientation

Attend mandatory orientation program for parents and guardians.

7

Final Enrollment

Complete enrollment process and receive official student ID and materials.

Balik-Aral Program

The Balik-Aral Program is a DepEd initiative designed to help out-of-school youth and adults continue their secondary education.

Program Features

Flexible Schedule

Classes available during evenings and weekends to accommodate working students

Modified Curriculum

Adapted curriculum focusing on essential competencies for adult learners

Age-Appropriate

Separate classes for adult learners (18 years and above)

Accreditation

Recognized diploma upon completion, equivalent to regular high school diploma

Eligibility Requirements

Age Requirement

At least 18 years old (for adult learners program)

Last Grade Completed

Must have completed at least Grade 6 or higher

Years Out of School

At least one year out of formal schooling

Medical Certificate

Health clearance for adult learners

Required Documents

Birth Certificate

PSA-authenticated copy

Last School Attended Record

Form 137 or equivalent from last school attended

Barangay Certification

Proof of residency and out-of-school status

2x2 ID Pictures

4 copies with white background
