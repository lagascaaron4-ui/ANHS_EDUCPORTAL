Languages & Communication

Progressive curriculum in General Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics from basic to advanced concepts.

Special Programs & Opportunities

Enhanced learning experiences beyond the standard curriculum

Special Program in the Arts (SPA)

For artistically gifted students

Specialized training in visual arts, theater, music, dance, and creative writing. Partnership with local artists and cultural institutions.

Requirements: Audition/Portfolio Review

Grades: 7-10

Special Program in Sports (SPS)

For athletically talented students

Intensive training in various sports while maintaining academic excellence. Opportunities to compete at regional and national levels.

Requirements: Sports Tryouts/Competition Records

Grades: 7-10

Special Science Class (SSC)

For academically advanced students

Accelerated science and mathematics curriculum with enhanced research opportunities and academic competitions.

Requirements: Entrance Exam & Interview

Grades: 7-10

Admission Requirements

General requirements for enrollment in ANHS programs

For New Students (Grade 7 & Transferees)

Report Card (Form 138)

Original copy of the most recent report card with at least 75% general average.

Birth Certificate

PSA-authenticated birth certificate (original and photocopy).

Good Moral Certificate

Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school.

2x2 ID Pictures

Four recent identical 2x2 ID photos with white background.

Medical Certificate

Health certificate from a licensed physician.

Proof of Residency

Barangay clearance or any proof of residence in Aliaga.
