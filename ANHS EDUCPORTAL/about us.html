About ANHS

Building a legacy of excellence in public education for over 25 years

Our Story

A journey of dedication, growth, and educational excellence

Aliaga National High School (ANHS) was established in 1995 as a public secondary school in the municipality of Aliaga, Nueva Ecija. What began as a small school with just 200 students and 10 teachers has grown into one of the premier public high schools in the province, serving over 2,500 students from Grades 7 to 10.

Our mission is to provide quality, accessible, and relevant education to the youth of Aliaga and neighboring communities. We are committed to developing well-rounded individuals who are academically competent, morally upright, and socially responsible.

ANHS implements the DepEd K to 12 Basic Education Program with a focus on Junior High School education (Grades 7-10). Our curriculum equips learners with the knowledge, skills, and values needed for future academic and life success.

  • DepEd-recognized public secondary school
  • Accredited to offer Special Program in Science (SPA) and Special Program in Sports (SPS)
  • Consistent top performer in Division and Regional academic competitions
  • Active partner in community development and outreach programs
  • Committed to inclusive education and learner-centered teaching

Our History

Milestones that shaped our journey

1995

Foundation

Aliaga National High School was established with initial enrollment of 200 students and 10 teachers. First campus located in Barangay Poblacion East.

2005

Expansion Phase

Construction of additional classroom buildings to accommodate growing enrollment. Introduction of computer laboratory and library facilities.

2010

Special Programs

Implementation of Special Program in Science (SPA) and Special Program in Sports (SPS) to cater to students with exceptional talents.

2016

K-12 Transition

Successful transition to the Enhanced Basic Education Curriculum. Full implementation of Junior High School program (Grades 7-10).

2020

Modernization Phase

Digital transformation initiatives. Enhancement of science laboratories and integration of technology in teaching and learning.

2024

Excellence Continues

ANHS continues to be a beacon of quality public education, producing graduates who excel in their next academic level and beyond.

Our Facilities

Modern infrastructure supporting quality education

Academic Buildings

Multiple classroom buildings equipped with modern furniture, ventilation, and lighting to provide a conducive learning environment for all students.

Computer Laboratory

Well-equipped computer lab with internet connectivity, supporting digital literacy and technology-integrated learning across all grade levels.

Library & Learning Center

Comprehensive library with diverse collection of books, reference materials, and reading spaces to support research and independent learning.

Science Laboratories

Specialized science labs for Biology, Chemistry, and Physics with modern equipment for hands-on experiments and practical learning.

MAPEH Facilities

Dedicated spaces for Music, Arts, Physical Education, and Health including music room, art studio, and covered court for sports activities.

School Grounds

Spacious campus with green spaces, gardens, and open areas for outdoor activities, assemblies, and student recreation.
